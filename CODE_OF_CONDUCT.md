# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We aim to keep this project welcoming, practical, and respectful for contributors and users.

## Expected Behavior

- Be considerate and direct.
- Assume good intent while asking for clarification when needed.
- Keep discussion focused on the project and its users.
- Respect maintainers' time and decisions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, abuse, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing project discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict participation for behavior that violates this code of conduct.
